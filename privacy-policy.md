# Overwatch Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

This explains what data Overwatch ("the Bot") handles.

## Data the Bot stores
Stored in a file on the Bot's host:
- **Server settings:** server ID, log channel ID, welcome channel ID.
- **Warnings:** the warned user's ID, the reason given, the moderator's ID, and the time of the warning.

## Data the Bot processes but does not store
- **Message events:** when logging is enabled, the Bot reads deleted and edited messages (author, channel, content) and forwards them to the server's chosen log channel. The Bot does not keep its own copy.
- **Member events:** joins and leaves (username, ID, account creation date) are posted to the log channel.
- **Command inputs** such as the user, reason and duration you provide.

## How the data is used
Only to run moderation, logging and welcome features inside the server where it was collected. We don't sell, share or use it for advertising or AI training.

## Retention and deletion
- Warnings and settings are kept until a moderator clears them (`/clearwarnings`, `/setlog`, `/setwelcome`) or the Bot is removed and the data deleted.
- To request deletion of your data, contact us using the details below.
- Log messages posted in a server's log channel are controlled by that server's admins.

## Children
Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in their country). The Bot does not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.

## Changes
We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
